Event direction

Papahānaumokuākea is a place of deep cultural and ecological importance. But for many, it feels far away. Our goal is to bridge that distance through community-led conversations that build connection, awareness, and stewardship.

Working format

The event opens with welcoming everyone at 5:30 PM, followed by cultural protocol at 5:45 PM and pūpū at 6:00 PM. Guests begin entering the Sphere around 6:15 PM, while the tables, Tunnel, keiki room and outdoor areas remain available. Talk Story finishes around 7:30–7:45 PM, then continues informally outside until 8:30 PM.
